# Starbits
## Information
Starbits is a small datapack that adds meteor showers to your world! There is a 25% chance for each night to have a meteor shower, spawning meteors in front of every player! You may also see large meteor clusters throughout the night! Additionally, meteors come in different colors inspired by real life meteors!

**Note:** It is reccomended that you have at least Render Distance & Simulation Distance of 14 and 200% Entity Distance for the best looking meteors!
